WPA3 (Personal / Enterprise) RECOMMENDED
Uses SAE (Dragonfly) handshake, resists offline dictionary guessing, and provides 192-bit security suite option.
WPA2 (AES / CCMP) SOLID
Strong encryption when paired with a long, unique passphrase and WPS disabled; avoid legacy TKIP.
WEP BROKEN
Cryptographically broken (RC4 IV weaknesses); can be cracked rapidly. Upgrade router immediately.
Open Wi-Fi UNENCRYPTED
No wireless encryption; traffic is exposed to local eavesdropping unless protected by HTTPS + trusted VPN.
